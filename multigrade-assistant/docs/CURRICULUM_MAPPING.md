## Verification Criteria
1. **Curriculum Fit:** Ensure the learning outcome in `curriculum.ts` matches the official textbook competency statement.
2. **Pedagogical Sequence:** Confirm the grade progression matches the textbook chapter order.
3. **No Fabrications:** Entries must remain marked `'to-verify'` until matched with an edition-verified physical textbook.
4. **Native-Speaker Review:** Hindi and Marathi terms must be reviewed with practicing primary school teachers in Maharashtra before field deployment.
